# Assessment policy — verified 25 September 2026

Sources:
- [LNR Delay Repay](https://www.londonnorthwesternrailway.co.uk/about-us/delay-repay)
- [Avanti Delay Repay](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/help-and-support/delay-repay)
- [Avanti cancellation guidance](https://www.avantiwestcoast.co.uk/travel%20information/disruptions/cancelled-train)

Both operators use destination delay against the advertised timetable, a 15-minute threshold, valid travel evidence, and a 28-day claim deadline. Apply to the operator causing the initial disruption. Unused journeys belong to the refund process.

The LNR page reviewed does **not** establish a universal rule that the fastest available train replaces the arrival of the train actually taken. Therefore we do not invent one. A faster alternative raises a review question when the journey taken is unknown; after confirmation, use the actual arrival of the selected train relative to the intended service's advertised arrival.

Cancellation ticket acceptance is conditional. LNR lists certain other operators within two hours, excluding advance-advertised engineering/timetable changes. Avanti also publishes cancellation acceptance guidance. RTT alone cannot establish ticket validity, advance notice, ability to board, or whether the passenger travelled. Cross-operator options are never silently accepted or silently treated as impossible.

## Application decisions (not additional operator terms)

- Every monitored departure is a **candidate intended journey**, never proof that the owner travelled.
- Completed original train, no earlier-arriving candidate in the collected set: potential if delay meets threshold, explicitly conditional on having used that train with a valid ticket.
- Earlier-arriving alternatives and unknown journey: needs review. Do not mechanically subtract time or declare a claim.
- Cancellation, missing actual, changed calling pattern, incomplete collection, unknown operator or pre-verification journey: needs review.
- Confirmed original/alternative journey and confirmed ticket validity: use that service's recorded actual arrival against the intended advertised arrival. Alternative departure must be at/after intended departure.
- Choosing not to travel: no Delay Repay claim; seek a refund.
- For cancellations, an earliest-arrival scenario may be shown as an estimate; it is not proof of the passenger's delay.
- Alternatives span one hour before the monitoring window through four hours after its end; actual departures, boarding/alighting calls and direction are checked. It is not an exhaustive connection planner. No interchanges or buses are inferred.
- Acknowledgement freezes the displayed claim assessment. Refreshes still append new assessments; Undo restores the pre-acknowledgement state, and explicit reassessment can apply newer evidence.
- Records older than 28 days remain searchable. Deadline warning does not destroy or relabel historical evidence.
- Unsupported rule periods return needs review. Rule versions are immutable; new policy needs a new version and tests.

A potential flag is a prompt to verify and submit **one claim for the journey actually made**, not permission to claim for every disrupted service in a window. The app never submits a claim.
